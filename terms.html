Terms of use

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Find My Pet Cyprus is a free community noticeboard. Please keep listings honest, kind and about pets.

Posting a listing

  • Only post pets that are genuinely missing, found or in need of a new home.
  • Make sure the information and photos are accurate and that you have the right to share them.
  • Do not include other people’s personal details without their permission.
  • Close your listing as soon as the pet is home, rehomed or no longer needs help.

Not allowed

  • Selling or breeding animals. Adoption listings may ask only for a reasonable fee to cover vaccinations, microchipping or neutering.
  • Spam, adverts, duplicate listings or false information.
  • Offensive, threatening or misleading content.

Moderation

Every listing is checked by a moderator before it is published. We may ask for changes, refuse or remove any listing, and suspend accounts that break these rules.

Staying safe

  • Never pay money to someone who says they have found your pet before you have seen it. This is a common scam.
  • Meet in a public place and take someone with you.
  • Ask a vet to scan a found pet for a microchip, and report lost pets to your municipality and the police.

Our responsibility

We provide the website as it is and cannot guarantee that a pet will be found, or that the information posted by others is correct. We are not responsible for arrangements made between users.

Law
